- *The walk.* `pushLanding` (`src/sim/push.ts`) ends before any cell
  whose `costAt` is Infinity: the board's edge, `chasm` and `deep_water`,
  the only impassable tile kinds (`src/sim/TileGrid.ts`). A fall is a
  change at that one check, telling the edge from the two tiles. Scenery
  and a body larger than one cell never move, so no wall or big beast
  falls. The Snag's room gate calls the same walk
  (`pullHasRoom`, `propose.ts`), so a drop counts as room once the walk
  returns it.
- *The seam for the drop.* `World.forceMove` puts the victim on its
  landing at once and holds it for the slide (`ForcedMoveAction`), whose
  `impact` runs the landing tile's enter effects
  (`World.applyTileEnterEffects`) as the slide ends: a drop there follows
  the look. The action carries no data today (`toData` returns `{}`), so
  crediting the pusher at the slide's end means serializing its id.
- *Death and credit.* There is one death path, `reapUnit`, run by
  `reapDead` at the tick's end; `unit:died` names no killer. Credit rides
  on damage: `dealDamage` books the XP ledger (`recordDamage`; XP is
  damage dealt plus a flat slice, `src/sim/xp.ts`), marks a camp hostile
  and stamps its `killedBy`; `applyDamage` fires `kill`, which the Pact of
  Dis Pater hooks (2 bits a kill). A fall booked through `dealDamage` with
  the victim's remaining HP, defense bypassed, is credited as a blow
  would be, with no `unit:attacked` and so no hitsplat.
- *Bosses.* No unit is marked a boss. A boss encounter fields ordinary
  archetypes, and its boss is the spec with a `power` override: the
  Bandit King's adventurer (6), the Bandit Queen's banshee (3), the
  Generalissimo's officer (3), the Witch Hunt's luminant (6), the Divine
  Bear's bear (4, one a stage). Those archetypes also fight as ordinary
  units, so the field goes on the wave's unit spec
  (`src/config/encounters.ts`), through `resolveWave`
  (`src/run/encounters/wave.ts`) to the template and the unit. The
  encounter editor's writer (`tools/encounter-editor/format.ts`) must
  carry it.
- *Snapshots.* `Unit` and the spawn queue's templates serialize, and so
  does the forced move's action: World v40 → v41 (the flag) and v42 (the
  pusher), or one bump if they land together. No RunSnapshot field: the
  templates are resolved at the battle's start and the run keeps no
  battle.
- *The look and the sound.* A death fades its glyph over `FADE_SECONDS`
  (`BattleRenderer.onUnitDied`) with the `death` cue
  (`src/audio/eventSounds.ts`); cues are generated by
  `scripts/gen-sfx.mjs`, so a drop's and a splash's are cheap.
- *Where it shows.* Chasm on `junctionAmbush` and `spiralFireLife`, deep
  water on `isthmus` and `icebergs`, and the procedural maps' pools in
  the tundra and swamp themes (`deepWaterFraction`). Gotcha #73 stands: a
  chasm stays impassable to every path and invisible to line of sight;
  only a fall enters it.

### 160.5b — the fall (2026-11-20)

`pushLanding` takes `drops`: a tile that drops (`TileDef.drops`, on
`chasm` and `deep_water`) ends the walk on it, the landing marked
`drops`; a body or a claim on that tile still stops the walk before it,
and a victim already standing on one moves no further. The push op
passes `drops` for every victim but the caster and a boss; the pull's
room gate passes it too, so a drop counts as room. `World.forceMove`
takes the pusher, which `ForcedMoveAction` carries and serializes (World
v42), and its `impact` lands the unit (`World.landForcedMove`): on a
tile that drops, `unit:fell`, then `dealDamage` for every HP it has,
defense bypassed, so the pusher's XP ledger, a camp's hostility and its
kill stamp book the fall as a blow's; then the pusher's `kill` hooks;
no `unit:attacked` and no `dealHit`. A victim a blow killed during its
slide is that blow's. Until 160.5c a fall looks and sounds like any
death; `unit:fell` waits as a sound `candidate`. Gotcha #142 records the
unit standing on the tile for its slide.

`src/sim/fall.test.ts`, worked by hand on the grid: a knockback walks a
foe onto a chasm, where it stands alive for the slide and falls as it
ends, the caster credited with its HP and no hit drawn; the pusher
survives a snapshot taken mid-slide; deep water drowns as chasm drops;
a pull drags a foe into the gap, so its gate counted the drop; the kill
hooks fire and a camp turns hostile; a boss stops at the edge; the
board's edge stops the walk; without `drops` the walk stops before the
tiles; a second victim stops at a falling one's tile, and a falling one
moves no further. 13 plants, all caught, control clean
(`scratch/160.5/160.5b-plants.json`), among them the drop at the push
rather than the slide's end, the fall drawn as a hit, and the board's
edge dropping too.

Corpus: predicted 5 to 25 of 34; 1 diverged (`player-3`). A count of
the replays (`scratch/160.5/corpus-falls.ts`): 70 forced moves in the
whole corpus and 2 falls, both in `player-3`, the journal that moved, so
the miss is the rate, not a fall that fails to fire. The prediction
counted journals with a pusher on a dropping map, and few of the
corpus's runs field a pusher at all. How often falls happen in play is
160.5d's to measure. Re-recorded; 0 of 34 differ on the replay after;
`player-3` differs beyond its config hash. The chaos census passes.

### 160.5c — the look and the sound, built for the stop (2026-11-20)

A fall is drawn in place of a death's fade: `unit:fell` plays the cue
and marks the unit, and the `unit:died` its tick's reap emits draws the
glyph leaving through its tile (`src/render/fallMotion.ts`, `fallPose`,
driven per frame by `BattleRenderer.updateFalls`; the HP bar fades over
the same time). The slide has already carried the glyph down to the
tile's surface (a chasm's top at −1.2, water's at −0.4), and the
terrain's top face hides what sinks below it. Two arms each, switched by
the board panel's `fall` dial (`bp=fall-b`), a dev seam that goes when
one is signed:

- chasm `a`, the plunge: down 2 cells on gravity's curve, a third
  smaller, fading over the last 30%, 0.55 s; chasm `b`, the fall away:
  shrinks to a point while barely sinking, 0.7 s.
- deep water `a`, the sink: under slowly with a faint ripple of eight
  motes easing out along the surface, 0.9 s; deep water `b`, the splash:
  under fast, eight sparks of spray thrown up and out, 0.45 s.

The cues are generated (`scripts/gen-sfx.mjs`): `fall`, a whistle
dropping in pitch over a thin rush of air; `splash`, a slap of spray and
a low gulp. Each plays beside the death's cry, which every fall still
has. The regeneration left the other twelve cues byte-identical.

`src/render/fallMotion.test.ts` pins what every arm must do (start as
the glyph stands, only go down, end unseen) and what tells each pair
apart. In the pane (Chromium, so wiring, not feel), on the spiral and
the isthmus: a staged forced move dropped its victim at the slide's end
(`unit:fell` at tick 5 for a push at tick 2), the sprite's height and
alpha matched `fallPose` frame by frame in each of the four arms, the
sprite and its handle were gone at the end, each water arm spawned its
eight sparks, and the audio heard `fall` or `splash` then `death` per
fall; no console errors. Falls also happened unstaged: one on the
spiral's second battle, and on the isthmus, with every battle forced
there, 21 in a seed's four battles, all enemies, 19 of them thrown by
the player's grenadiers (16 giant rats). That rate is a staged run's,
every battle on the water map; 160.5d's census is the measure.

**Signed** ("I'm signing all of those! 😁 That is awesome, and
hilarious, and feels incredibly satisfying!", 2026-11-20): the chasm's
plunge (`a`), deep water's splash (`b`), both cues as built, and the
