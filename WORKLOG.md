### The kickoff's audit, and the cut put to the user (2026-11-20)

Read at `fa2bfd61`. Also signed at §160's close: the CRLF fact line in
`process/shell-and-windows.md` (`fa2bfd61`, checked first: text mode
wrote CRLF, binary mode and `newline` LF).

- *The walk.* `pushLanding` (`src/sim/push.ts`) ends before any cell
  whose `costAt` is Infinity: the board's edge, `chasm` and `deep_water`,
  the only impassable tile kinds (`src/sim/TileGrid.ts`). A fall is a
  change at that one check, telling the edge from the two tiles. Scenery
  and a body larger than one cell never move, so no wall or big beast
  falls. The Snag's room gate calls the same walk
  (`pullHasRoom`, `propose.ts`), so a drop counts as room once the walk
  returns it.
- *The seam for the drop.* `World.forceMove` puts the victim on its
  landing at once and holds it for the slide (`ForcedMoveAction`), whose
  `impact` runs the landing tile's enter effects
  (`World.applyTileEnterEffects`) as the slide ends: a drop there follows
  the look. The action carries no data today (`toData` returns `{}`), so
  crediting the pusher at the slide's end means serializing its id.
- *Death and credit.* There is one death path, `reapUnit`, run by
  `reapDead` at the tick's end; `unit:died` names no killer. Credit rides
  on damage: `dealDamage` books the XP ledger (`recordDamage`; XP is
  damage dealt plus a flat slice, `src/sim/xp.ts`), marks a camp hostile
  and stamps its `killedBy`; `applyDamage` fires `kill`, which the Pact of
  Dis Pater hooks (2 bits a kill). A fall booked through `dealDamage` with
  the victim's remaining HP, defense bypassed, is credited as a blow
  would be, with no `unit:attacked` and so no hitsplat.
- *Bosses.* No unit is marked a boss. A boss encounter fields ordinary
  archetypes, and its boss is the spec with a `power` override: the
  Bandit King's adventurer (6), the Bandit Queen's banshee (3), the
  Generalissimo's officer (3), the Witch Hunt's luminant (6), the Divine
  Bear's bear (4, one a stage). Those archetypes also fight as ordinary
  units, so the field goes on the wave's unit spec
  (`src/config/encounters.ts`), through `resolveWave`
  (`src/run/encounters/wave.ts`) to the template and the unit. The
  encounter editor's writer (`tools/encounter-editor/format.ts`) must
  carry it.
- *Snapshots.* `Unit` and the spawn queue's templates serialize, and so
  does the forced move's action: World v40 → v41 (the flag) and v42 (the
  pusher), or one bump if they land together. No RunSnapshot field: the
  templates are resolved at the battle's start and the run keeps no
  battle.
- *The look and the sound.* A death fades its glyph over `FADE_SECONDS`
  (`BattleRenderer.onUnitDied`) with the `death` cue
  (`src/audio/eventSounds.ts`); cues are generated by
  `scripts/gen-sfx.mjs`, so a drop's and a splash's are cheap.
- *Where it shows.* Chasm on `junctionAmbush` and `spiralFireLife`, deep
  water on `isthmus` and `icebergs`, and the procedural maps' pools in
  the tundra and swamp themes (`deepWaterFraction`). Gotcha #73 stands: a
  chasm stays impassable to every path and invisible to line of sight;
  only a fall enters it.
